# Model selection protocol

Public extract of the team's dated pre-registration and amendments. Dates refer to the working record. The single-commit release is a later snapshot.

**8 November.** Comparisons use complete matches held out from training, the same splits for each candidate, equal-weight probabilities and the fixed half-wise 20/32 decoder. To test the sampler, compare the nine-member C ensemble with the six-member B ensemble and with B plus three ordinary seeds. Attribute an improvement to short-spell sampling only if it beats the extra-seed control. For additional members, compare C plus the candidate with C plus a new plain seed, each at one-tenth weight. The original paired gate on v7c and v6c required a mean gain of at least 0.003, wins on at least 8 of 13 and a 90% bootstrap interval above zero. The register recorded a secondary choice among marginal candidates passing the do-no-harm check.

**Registered external veto.** Once, on 20 SkillCorner matches, revert a selected member if the mean falls by 0.003 or more against its control. This check could discard a choice. A positive result could not promote another candidate or change a setting. [Veto results](skillcorner-veto-2026-11-10.md)

**9 November amendment.** For subsequent extra members, require non-negative paired means on v5bfc, v7c and v6c, both against C and against the mean of three extra-seed controls. For subsequent training changes, require at least +0.003 on the worst of the three copies against the matched recipe. Apply comparisons per half, with the whole match as the unit of replication. Width had already been judged under the earlier rule.

**13 November amendment.** A separate 12/32 decoder was conditional on explicit written permission from the organisers before the freeze to use a published benchmark diagnostic for choosing label conventions. The locked manifest shipped here uses the registered fallback, 20/32. [Manifest](../weights/manifest.json)
