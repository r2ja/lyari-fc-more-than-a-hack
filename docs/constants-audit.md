# Entry D settings and their origin

This is the release extract of the team's Entry D constants audit, including the sampler confirmation of 12 November. The four choices examined using possession labels were the training hold, network recipe, decoder and ensemble membership.

- **Labels.** An intervening team spell shorter than 38 frames can revert to the surrounding team. On 16 October, before the organisers' 23 October clarification that the public window is not a tuning set, we chose 1.5 seconds to match the possession rate and mean spell length in the published five minutes. On 28 October a first open-data analysis of seven Sportec matches favoured 3.0–3.8 seconds; a length-bias correction, added after that result, gave 1.5 seconds on the five Sportec training matches using DFL events, and we kept it. The transformation is in [labels.py](../lyari/dataset/labels.py).
- **Tracking.** Distances use metres and elapsed time uses 25 frames per second. A frozen player slot, an isolated implausible ball jump and a goalkeeper parked in the other team’s penalty area can corrupt team shape. [quirks.py](../lyari/pipeline/quirks.py) applies physical rules. Enabling them followed a label-free diagnostic on the competition tracking and a check on the open copies.
- **Ball repair.** The maximum filled gap is 8 seconds. Confidence falls with gap duration as `1 / (1 + 0.65 × gap_seconds)` inside a bounded gap. The scale was estimated from straight-line interpolation error on external training matches. [Ball repair](../lyari/models/rules/ball_repair.py)
- **Control physics.** Three members use relative player–ball velocity over 0.48 seconds and a proximity score with scales of 2.5 m and 4 m/s. On v5bfc practice frames meeting the audit’s visibility and confidence criteria, the team with the higher score had possession 91.0% of the time. [Player features](../lyari/models/players/features.py)
- **Optimisation.** The model uses a set encoder and bidirectional GRU, windows of 500 frames, 30 epochs of 200 batches, a batch size of 32, and exponential moving averaging at 0.999. Development used external practice labels. Some early practice copies used simulation parameters informed by unlabelled competition tracking. All ten final fits used the external v5bf simulator. [Complete recipe](../lyari/jobs/recipe.json)
- **Short-spell sampler.** Three members centre 25% of windows on complete possessions lasting at most 5 seconds. The 5-second cutoff followed short-spell error analysis on open data. Both values were set before the first sampler score. The remaining six standard-width models use the plain or control-physics recipe.
- **Width and averaging.** Nine members use hidden width 128 and one uses 256. All ten have equal weight. Selection used paired comparisons on v5bfc, v7c and v6c and the registered external veto. Statistics from the unlabelled competition tracking informed v7c and v6c. The v7c copy also retained assumptions about stoppages informed by PFF. [Damage provenance](damage-v5be.md) · [Veto results](skillcorner-veto-2026-11-10.md)
- **Decoder.** The H↔A switch cost is 20 and the cost to enter or leave D is 32. These were first picked on 28 October on practice copies (v5bc, v5c) whose simulated tracking loss was fitted to label-free aggregates of the competition tracking, and re-checked on 6 November on the external v5bfc copy. 20/32 was not the best cell there; the best alternative differed by an amount within the paired uncertainty. The final decoder resets at halftime. [Selection protocol](prereg-2026-11-08.md)

The [open-data hold analysis](min-hold-sportec.md) records the uncertainty in the 38-frame rule. The [damage provenance](damage-v5be.md) covers the external camera-loss and spatial-error settings. The [manifest](../weights/manifest.json) identifies the exact checkpoints and decoder used.
